III. The House

Two Crafts, One Hour.

Noir & Aurum began with a simple observation: a watch marks the hour, but a scent is what people remember about it. We build both under one roof so neither is ever an afterthought to the other.

Our Instinct

Dark Dials, Warm Gold.

Our watches favour dark dials, warm gold detailing, and mechanisms left uncluttered. Our fragrances follow the same instinct — a little smoke, a little warmth, nothing shouted.

We are a small house, still being built in the open. What you see here today is the beginning of that build.

An Honest Word

The collections are being finished by hand, chapter by chapter. We'd rather show you the house as it's actually built than pretend it arrived whole.

What Guides Us

Four Ideas, One House.

Restraint

No clutter on the dial, no excess in the case. We remove until only what earns its place remains.

Craft

Small numbers, finished by hand, chapter by chapter — not rushed to meet a season.

Patience

A collection prepared to order. We would rather arrive late and right than early and ordinary.

Warmth

Dark dials and warm gold, smoked woods and quiet amber — one house style through both crafts.

“Gold catches the light. Smoke keeps the secret.”

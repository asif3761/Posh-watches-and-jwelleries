The House

Two Crafts, One Hour.

Noir & Aurum began with a simple observation: a watch marks the hour, but a scent is what people remember about it. We build both under one roof so they're never an afterthought to each other.

Our watches favour dark dials, warm gold detailing, and mechanisms left uncluttered. Our fragrances follow the same instinct — a little smoke, a little warmth, nothing shouted.

We are a small house, still being built in the open. What you see here today is the beginning of that build — the collections are being finished by hand, chapter by chapter.

"Gold catches the light. Smoke keeps the secret."
